Dr. Anurag (Ari) Goel is a double board-certified psychiatrist with over 15 years of experience providing compassionate, expert psychiatric care to adults with serious mental illness in Los Angeles.

Dr. Goel specializes in the treatment of schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder, severe bipolar disorder, and severe major depression, including those with co-occurring substance use disorders. He treats all patients, including those experiencing homelessness and housing instability.

He provides in-person psychiatric services at the Long Beach Multi-Service Center. He also offers virtual and telephone appointments for patients throughout Los Angeles County.
